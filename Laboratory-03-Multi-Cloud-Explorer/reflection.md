Mission Reflection

After exploring AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, the platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud because of its strong focus on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes. I also found its services easy to relate to modern application development because tools such as Compute Engine, Cloud Storage, Google Kubernetes Engine, BigQuery, and Vertex AI support many modern workloads.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have several similarities. All three provide virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity and access management, managed databases, Kubernetes services, security tools, and global cloud infrastructure. However, each platform also has its own strengths. AWS has a broad range of mature cloud services, Azure integrates well with Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud is known for its strengths in artificial intelligence, data analytics, and cloud-native applications.

I learned that business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud provider. A company should not choose a platform simply because it is popular. For example, an organization already using Windows Server and Microsoft technologies may benefit from Azure, while an AI research company may prefer Google Cloud.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to consider factors such as scalability, security, compatibility, performance, and business needs before recommending a solution. Different organizations may require different cloud platforms depending on their goals.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved because I added organized research documents, comparison tables, client recommendations, Linux investigation results, screenshots, and reflection. It now provides clearer evidence of what I learned during the activity.
